What We Do

Reads the rig.

We take second-by-second EDR from Pason and NOV and label what the rig was doing: drilling, sliding, tripping, running casing. We call this reading the data, and we never claim more than it shows.

Remembers every well.

Each stand, connection and bit run is compared with your own best, not with someone else's fleet.

Holds your files. in demo

You connect your Drive or OneDrive folder, read-only. Every file is catalogued, and the survey, daily, mud and bit-run tables are pulled out with a link back to the source page. Each well gets a proposed list of files it should have, with the missing ones ranked worst first.

How It Works

  1. 1

    Connect the EDR and your well folder. We only read them and never write to them.

  2. 2

    We build one record per well: rig activity, stands, runs and files.

  3. 3

    Ask a question. Every number in the answer cites the file or page it came from.

Your Data Stays Yours

Each client has its own fenced data, and your thresholds are fitted to your wells. Your data is never used in another client's work, examples or models.
